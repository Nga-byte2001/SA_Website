Joint-School Activities 聯校活動

Christmas Ball 聖誕舞會

A joint-school Christmas celebration with TLGS, Queen Elizabeth School, and Cheung Sha Wan Catholic School, featuring performances, games, and festive activities.

Exam Paper Exchanges 試卷交換

Inter-school past paper exchanges for IB and DSE students. Sharing resources to help everyone prepare better.

Music Show 音樂表演

Joint-school music performances showcasing talent across schools. A night of music and connection.

Sport Competition 體育比賽

Inter-school sport competitions fostering friendly rivalry and camaraderie between schools.

Joint-School Oral Practices 聯校口語練習

Collaborative oral practice sessions with partner schools to enhance language skills and confidence.

HK Union of Young Leaders 香港青年領袖同盟

We suggest the school join HKUYL, a student-based non-profit that nurtures leadership through seminars, social service projects, and training camps. Promoting global citizenship among SSC students.
